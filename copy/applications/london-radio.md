## Tell us about yourself!

I'm Aureliano, I DJ as elmozzo. I'm from Italy and I run Supervuoto, a radio
show that's slowly turned into a small collective of DJs, photographers and
friends. Every episode I invite someone and we each play a set around the same
theme. The guests are people I find interesting, not necessarily known names.
So far it's been my old partner from Inedia, a 16 year old girl into Frank
Ocean and MF DOOM, a friend from punk who now makes dark ambient, a dnb DJ from
Belgium, and a few others. All the covers are shot on film.

I'm also a developer, so I built the website (supervuoto.org) and a little 3D
player called wanderer where you can walk around while a mix plays.

## Why do you want to host a show?

I already do it in Italy and it's the thing I enjoy most. I like the radio
format more than just uploading mixes: there's a time, people tune in, and you
have to make choices for them.

London is where a lot of the music I play comes from, so it makes sense to try
it there. I'd like to keep the same format and start inviting London people as
guests, mixed with the Italian ones.

## What is your show proposal?

Supervuoto: one hour, me and a guest. I play a set, they play a set, both on the
same theme. Each episode has a title and a film photo for the cover, and the
tracklist goes up on supervuoto.org afterwards.

Genres move a lot from episode to episode, and that's on purpose. So far we've
covered UK garage, dubstep and deep bass, drum & bass, boom bap and beats,
cumbia and dembow, psytrance, Arab electronic, acid, electro and techno. What
ties it together is the mood more than the genre.

Guests: DJs, producers and selectors with a clear taste of their own, often
people who don't get many chances to play on radio. Young people, friends from
other scenes (punk, metal, hip hop), people who've never done a mix before. In
London I'd like to invite local DJs and keep a few Italian guests in the
rotation.

Segments:
- Supervuoto BIT, the main format: elmozzo VS guest.
- Background radiation: an ambient / drone / noise episode now and then, good
  for a late slot. The first one is already being made.
- Monography: occasionally a full hour on one artist or one record label.
- Every show opens the same way, with a short spoken intro and a bell.
